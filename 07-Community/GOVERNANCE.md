# Governance

## Principles

- Transparent decisions.
- Public documentation.
- Respectful collaboration.
- Reproducible processes.
- No fabricated activity.
- No ownership claims over community contributions without clear licensing terms.

## Decision Records

Important architectural or product decisions should be recorded with:

- context;
- alternatives considered;
- decision;
- consequences;
- date;
- follow-up conditions.

## Maintainers

Maintainers are responsible for:

- reviewing contributions;
- protecting project quality;
- coordinating releases;
- resolving conflicts;
- documenting significant decisions.

## Project Independence

Projects may have different maintainers, licenses, technologies, and release schedules. Shared infrastructure should not become a hidden coupling requirement.
